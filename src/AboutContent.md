# About Elapsed

We’ve gotten used to dopamine being fast and constant.

We scroll through large amounts of content that isn’t even that interesting, mostly to reach the next small hit of novelty. Over time, this changes what our brain considers “normal”.

As a result, real life starts to feel boring. Patience drops. Simple or slow activities feel harder to start or less rewarding.

Elapsed is a thought experiment.

If too much dopamine makes life feel dull, what happens if an app removes it almost entirely?

Elapsed is a social media app where you can’t post, comment, or interact meaningfully. It comes with a fixed set of pointless videos. There’s no feed optimization, no payoff, and nothing to win.

The app tracks how much time you spend bored.

If social media apps showed this metric, most people would be surprised by how long they stay while feeling nothing in particular.

Elapsed exists to make that time visible.
